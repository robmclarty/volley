Write an essay that makes a case — for or against, but committed — on this
question: **does handwriting still earn its place in an age of keyboards?**

Save it as `essay.md` at the workspace root, 900–1400 words of continuous
prose.

Shape it like this:

- One `# ` title of your own making — a claim, not a label.
- An opening paragraph that ends in an arguable thesis: something a
  reasonable reader could push back on, not a description of the topic.
- At least three `## ` sections, each moving the argument forward. Ground
  every section in at least one concrete particular — a scene, a study, a
  named object. Somewhere in the body, state the strongest objection to your
  case fairly, then answer it.
- A closing section that returns to the thesis with the weight of the
  argument behind it.
- A final `## Sources` section listing at least three real, named works
  (author and title). Put each one to work in the body — cite it where it
  carries weight, don't let it decorate the end.

Write in paragraphs, not bullet points; list items belong only under
`## Sources`. Work in passes: draft the whole essay, then reread it and
revise before you call it done.

Before finishing, run `node check.mjs` with bash from the workspace root and
fix anything it reports — it must exit 0.
